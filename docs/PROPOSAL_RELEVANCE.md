# Proposal Relevance

Experiment 4 matters to the broader proposal because it shows one of the
clearest pipeline transitions in the stack:

- local simulation
- calibration-anchored model
- real backend contact

The repo does not claim more than it has. That is the point.

What it proves for the proposal:

- the team can preserve one narrow question across multiple evidence layers
- the team can attach a real backend rung without pretending that all layers
  are equally strong
- the stack is becoming more grounded, not more rhetorical

The best short read is:

`Experiment 4 now shows that the same narrow schedule-linked question can survive from simulation into calibration-anchored modeling and into direct FEZ backend ordering validation, even when the first real backend batch does not yet confirm the modeled advantage.`

That honesty is part of the value. The repo now demonstrates:

- a real backend contact layer
- a preserved evidence ladder
- a negative or neutral backend outcome retained rather than hidden
- a clear path toward `v4` as condition-finding rather than rhetoric
